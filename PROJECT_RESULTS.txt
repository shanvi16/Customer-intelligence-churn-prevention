CUSTOMER INTELLIGENCE & CHURN PREVENTION SYSTEM

Dataset:
IBM Telco Customer Churn

Dataset size:
7,043 rows and 21 original columns

Test set:
1,409 customers

Actual churn in test set:
374 customers (26.54%)

Models evaluated:
1. Logistic Regression
2. Decision Tree

Evaluation metrics:
Accuracy, Precision, Recall, F1-score, ROC-AUC

Business objective:
Prioritize customers by predicted churn risk to support
customer-retention outreach.

Limitations:
Predictions are estimates, not guarantees. Results are based
on the current test split and do not prove that retention
actions will reduce churn.
